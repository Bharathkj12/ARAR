ARAR GROUP is a premier holding company in the UAE, uniting leading specialized companies across Utility, Petrochemical, and Civil Infrastructure sectors.

With over 15 years of industry excellence, our group brings together hundreds of expert engineers and professionals to deliver world-class EPC (Engineering, Procurement & Construction) solutions. From complex high-voltage networks and vital petrochemical infrastructure to major road developments, we are committed to shaping the UAE's future with precision, safety, and unwavering quality.
