Who We Are

Engineering Excellence

ARAR Utility Co L.L.C is a Dubai-registered company specialized in EPC (Engineering, Procurement & Construction), encompassing LV, MV, HV, EHV and Street Lighting works along with associated structural works.

Our company comprises many professional engineers who have worked on major projects across Dubai for more than 15 years. We undertake projects per customer requirements and complete them within the time frame as per specifications and contract documents.
